## Trade-offs

| Consideration | Implication |
| --- | --- |
| Site compressibility | Compressible sites (IJ, femoral) tolerate lower thresholds than subclavian. |
| Urgency of access | Time-critical indications may justify proceeding without full correction. |
| Product exposure | Prophylactic FFP or platelets carry their own risks and often do not correct the abnormality. |
| Anticoagulants and antiplatelets | DOACs, warfarin, heparin and dual antiplatelet therapy each need separate consideration. |
